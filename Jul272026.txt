Purpose: This role was created to give project members the specific permissions they need to work within their subscription, without granting them broader built-in roles like Contributor or Owner. It follows least-privilege principles by scoping access to only the actions required for day-to-day project work.

Assignment model:
- The role is assigned at the subscription level to an Azure AD security group, not to individual users directly.
- Group membership is owned and managed by the respective Subscription Owner, who is accountable for knowing who's on their project and approving access.
- Cloud Operations defines and maintains the role itself (permissions, scope), while Subscription Owners manage who gets access through group membership — keeping role design and access approval clearly separated.
- This keeps access reviews and onboarding/offboarding centralized to the group, rather than requiring RBAC changes for every individual, which reduces the chance of stale or orphaned role assignments.
